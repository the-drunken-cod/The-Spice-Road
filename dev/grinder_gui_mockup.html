Spice Grinder GUI - layout mockup

One 280x248 px GUI (GUI scale 1) with the three kinds of screen the Grinder has. Numbers on the boxes are the GrinderLayout constants: x, y from the GUI's top left, w x h in GUI px. Everything not drawn here (the vanilla player inventory) sits at the standard 176 px wide spot, centred.

Zoom

1. Drafting - food chosen, spices clicked in the left list, nothing consumed yet

2. Running - the board, pawn on the centre, direction pad, points bars

3. Running - no moves left, accept needs a confirm (no effect earned), hovering an unknown cell
